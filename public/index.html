🏛️ About EDVANTA & MSOT

EDVANTA is the comprehensive academic progression tracker designed exclusively for the Mirai School of Technology (MSOT).

Our platform unifies course progression, syllabus pacing, and exam readiness across all three regional campuses: HI-Tech, Jagannath, and Rathinam.

With the founding motto "Every hour counts, every goal matters", EDVANTA empowers MSOT faculty and students with real-time curriculum intelligence, verified attendance workflows, and predictive academic readiness.
